Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Purpose

This is a personal setup used by one individual to back up OpenText Service Manager files to that individual's Google Drive using rclone.

Data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted, rclone may access Google Drive files, file contents, filenames, folder information, and related metadata needed to upload, list, and manage backups. Google OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration on the owner's server.

How data is used

Google Drive data is used only to perform backup and recovery operations requested by the owner. Files are transferred between the owner's server and the owner's Google Drive through Google's services.

Sharing

This personal setup does not operate a third-party data collection or advertising service. Backup files are not intentionally shared with other parties by this setup. Google processes data according to its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration. The owner is responsible for restricting access to the server, configuration file, backup files, and Google Drive account.
